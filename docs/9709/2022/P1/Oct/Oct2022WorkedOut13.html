Question 2 [2 + 1 + 3]

Original paper page 3

(a) [2] f(x) = −2x² − 8x − 13 (x < −3). Express f(x) in the form −2(x + a)² + b.

−2x² − 8x − 13 = −2(x² + 4x) − 13 = −2(x + 2)² + 8 − 13

f(x) = −2(x + 2)² − 5

(b) [1] Find the range of f.

For x < −3, (x + 2)² > 1, so −2(x + 2)² < −2 and f(x) < −7

f(x) < −7

(c) [3] Find an expression for f⁻¹(x).

y = −2(x + 2)² − 5 → (x + 2)² = −(y + 5)/2 x < −3 so x + 2 < −1: x = −2 − √(−(y + 5)/2)

f⁻¹(x) = −2 − √(−(x + 5)/2)

Question 4 [5]

Original paper page 5

A tank has volume V = 243 − (1/3)(9 − x)³ m³ at depth x m. Water enters at 3.6 m³ per hour. Find the rate of increase of depth when x = 4, in cm per minute.

dV/dx = (9 − x)² → at x = 4: dV/dx = 25 m² dx/dt = (dV/dt)/(dV/dx) = 3.6/25 = 0.144 m per hour 0.144 m/h = 14.4 cm/h = 14.4/60 = 0.24 cm/min

0.24 cm per minute

Question 8 [4 + 3]

Original paper page 10

(a) [4] Two identical circles of radius r with centres P and Q, PQ = (5/3)r, intersect at A and B. Find the perimeter of the shaded region in terms of r.

cos(∠APQ) = (PQ/2)/r = (5/6) → ∠APQ = cos⁻¹(5/6) = 0.5857 rad ∠APB = 2 × 0.5857 = 1.1713 rad Perimeter = 2 arcs of radius r, each subtending ∠APB: 2r(1.1713) = 2.34r

Perimeter ≈ 2.34r

Original paper page 11

(b) [3] Find the area of the shaded region in terms of r.

Shaded region = two equal segments. Each segment = ½r²(θ − sinθ), θ = 1.1713 Sector area = ½r²(1.1713) = 0.5857r²; triangle area = ½r²sin(1.1713) = 0.4606r² Segment = 0.5857r² − 0.4606r² = 0.1250r²; total = 2 × 0.1250r²

Area ≈ 0.250r²

Question 10 [4 + 4 + 2]

Original paper page 14

(a) [4] The circle x² + y² = 2 and the line y = 2x − 1 meet at A and B. Find the coordinates of A.

x² + (2x − 1)² = 2 → 5x² − 4x − 1 = 0 → (5x + 1)(x − 1) = 0 x = 1 gives y = 1; x = −1/5 gives B

A = (1, 1)

Original paper page 15

(b) [4] Find the volume of revolution of the shaded region (between x = 1 and x = √2, under the circle, above the x-axis) about the x-axis.

V = π∫₁^√2 (2 − x²) dx = π[2x − x³/3]₁^√2 = π[(2√2 − 2√2/3) − (2 − 1/3)] = π(4√2 − 5)/3

V = (π/3)(4√2 − 5)

(c) [2] Find an exact expression for the perimeter of the shaded region.

Boundary: AD = 1, x-axis from D(1, 0) to (√2, 0) = √2 − 1, and arc from A to (√2, 0), angle π/4, radius √2 → arc = π√2/4 Perimeter = 1 + (√2 − 1) + π√2/4

√2 + π√2/4 = √2(1 + π/4)
