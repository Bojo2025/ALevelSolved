Question 1 [5]

Coplanar forces of magnitudes P N, Q N, 16 N and 22 N act at a point and are in equilibrium. Find the values of P and Q.
Horizontal: P cos 25° = 22 + 16 cos 55° Vertical: Q + 16 sin 55° = P sin 25°
P = (22 + 16 cos 55°) / cos 25° = 34.4 Q = 34.40 sin 25° − 16 sin 55° = 1.43

P = 34.4 N, Q = 1.43 N

Question 2 [5]

Spheres A (6 kg) and B (2 kg) move towards each other at 5 m s⁻¹ and 3 m s⁻¹. After the collision both move in the same direction and their speeds differ by 2 m s⁻¹. Find the loss of kinetic energy.
Momentum: 6(5) + 2(−3) = 6vA + 2vB so 6vA + 2vB = 24 Speeds: vB = vA + 2 so vA = 2.5, vB = 4.5
KE before = 75 + 9 = 84 J KE after = 18.75 + 20.25 = 39 J

Loss of KE = 45 J

Question 3 [2 + 3 + 4]

(a) [2] Car at constant speed 28 m s⁻¹ on level road; resistance 1400 N. Find the power in kW.

Power = 1400 × 28 = 39 200 W

39.2 kW

(b) [3] Car (1250 kg) moves at constant speed up a hill with sin θ = 0.12, engine at 43.5 kW. Find the speed.

Resistance = 1400 + 1250(10)(0.12) = 2900 N 43 500 / v = 2900

v = 15 m s⁻¹

(c) [4] Car pulls a 600 kg trailer up the same hill. Driving force 5000 N, trailer resistance 300 N, car resistance 1400 N. Find the acceleration and the tension in the cable.

Whole system: 5000 − 1400 − 300 − 2220 = 1850a so a = 0.584 m s⁻² Trailer: T − 300 − 720 = 600a so T = 1370 N

a = 0.584 m s⁻², T = 1370 N

Question 4 [7 + 2]

(a) [7] Block of mass 8 kg on a rough plane inclined at 18°, pulled up by a string at 26° above a line of greatest slope, tension T. μ = 0.65. Acceleration is 0.2 m s⁻² up the plane. Find T.

Parallel: T cos 26° − 8g sin 18° − F = 8(0.2) Perpendicular: R = 8g cos 18° − T sin 26° F = 0.65R
Solving: 1.184T = 75.77 so T = 64.0

T = 64.0 N

(b) [2] The block starts from rest. Find the distance travelled in the fourth second.

s(4) − s(3) = ½(0.2)(16) − ½(0.2)(9) = 1.6 − 0.9

0.7 m

Question 5 [5 + 5]

(a) [5] Particle P starts at O with velocity −20 m s⁻¹, acceleration a = 12 − 2t. Sketch a velocity-time graph for 0 ≤ t ≤ 12, indicating when P is at rest.

v = 12t − t² − 20 = −(t − 2)(t − 10)

Graph: inverted parabola from (0, −20) to (12, −20), with maximum v = 16 at t = 6. Rest at t = 2 and t = 10.

(b) [5] Find the total distance travelled by P in 0 ≤ t ≤ 12.

x(t) = −20t + 6t² − t³/3 x(0) = 0, x(2) = −18.67, x(10) = 66.67, x(12) = 48
Distance = 18.67 + 85.33 + 18.67

≈ 123 m

Question 6 [6 + 6]

(a) [6] Particles A (4 kg) and B (3 kg) are joined by a light string over a smooth pulley at the top of a plane inclined at 30°. A hangs freely; B is on the plane. The plane is rough and the particles are in limiting equilibrium. Find the coefficient of friction between B and the plane.

A: T = 4g = 40 N B: T = 3g sin 30° + F, so F = 25 N, R = 3g cos 30° = 25.98 N

μ = 25 / 25.98 = 0.962

(b) [6] The plane is smooth. The particles are released from rest when the vertical height difference is 1 m. Use an energy method to find the speed when they are at the same horizontal level.

x + x sin 30° = 1 so x = 2/3 m 4g(2/3) − 3g(1/3) = ½(4 + 3)v² 26.67 − 10 = 3.5v²

v = 2.18 m s⁻¹
