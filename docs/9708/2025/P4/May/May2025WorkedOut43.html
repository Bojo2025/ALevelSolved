The article refers to structural unemployment. Explain what this means 3 and whether it is the same as the natural rate of unemployment. Structural unemployment occurs when the skills of the labour force are not equal to the jobs available. There are individuals seeking a job, but are not able to find one because they don't have the skills required or the number of jobs opened in an economy is lower than the number of people looking for a job.
